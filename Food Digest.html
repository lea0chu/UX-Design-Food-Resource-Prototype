Student recipe of the issue

5-minute soy-free protein bowl

Submitted by SR - Halal - Soy-free - ~$4 per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pre-cooked rice
  • 1 can chickpeas, drained
  • Olive oil, cumin, paprika
  • Lemon juice and salt

Steps

  1. Heat chickpeas in pan with oil and spices
  2. Warm rice in microwave 90 seconds
  3. Combine, squeeze lemon, done
