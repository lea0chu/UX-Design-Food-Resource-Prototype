Columbia Graduate Student Association

Food resources

A guide to campus, city, state, and federal support for graduate students.

These resources are available to you — no judgment, no referral needed for most. If you're unsure whether you qualify, check anyway. Many programs have broader eligibility than students expect.

🏛️ On campus

Columbia University Food Pantry

Free · No appt needed

Open to all Columbia students. Stocks shelf-stable foods, hygiene items, and sometimes fresh produce. No documentation required.

📍 Wien Hall

health.columbia.edu/food-pantry ↗

Graduate Dining Plan

Paid · Discounted rate

$11-$13.75 per swipe vs $17.50 walk-in. Eligible halls: Faculty House, Chef Mike's, Fac Shack. Flex dollars can be used on Grubhub delivery.

dining.columbia.edu · Contact OSA to enroll ↗

CU Basic Needs

Free · Confidential

Columbia's hub for food insecurity support. Connects students to the pantry, emergency funds, SNAP assistance, and counseling. Can help you navigate all other programs on this page.

studentaffairs.columbia.edu/basic-needs ↗

Emergency Student Funds

Application required

One-time grants for students facing unexpected financial hardship, including food insecurity. Administered by the Dean of Students office. Turnaround is typically within a week.

dos.columbia.edu/emergency-funds ↗

🇺🇸 Federal benefits

SNAP (Food Stamps)

Federal · Monthly benefit

Grad students who work 20+ hrs/week, or receive federal work-study, may qualify. Average benefit is $200+/month. US citizens and some visa holders are eligible — check your status at the link below.

mybenefits.ny.gov · otda.ny.gov/snap ↗

WIC (Women, Infants & Children)

Federal · If applicable

For pregnant, postpartum, or breastfeeding students, and children under 5. Provides monthly food benefits, nutrition support, and healthcare referrals. Income thresholds are generous.

health.ny.gov/wic ↗

🏙️ NYC & community programs

NYC Free Meals Program

Free · No ID required

NYC operates community kitchens and grab-and-go meal sites across all five boroughs. No income verification or ID needed. Find locations near campus or your home neighborhood.

nyc.gov/freemeals · Text "FOOD" to 877-877 ↗

Harlem food pantries

Free · Walk-in

Several pantries operate within walking distance of Morningside Heights. Includes options with halal-certified and culturally specific foods. Hours vary — check before visiting.

foodpantries.org · or call 311 ↗

Too Good To Go

App · $3-6 per bag

Rescue surplus food from restaurants, cafes, and bakeries near campus for $3-6. Highly active in the Columbia/UWS area. Grab bags are surprise assortments — great for cheap meals.

toogoodtogo.com · iOS & Android ↗

Plentiful app (NYC food pantry finder)

Free · Reserve ahead

Book pantry appointments across NYC from your phone. Shows real-time availability, wait times, and dietary filters including halal. Reduces time spent waiting in line.

plentifulapp.com ↗

🛒 Student discounts & savings

Morton Williams

10% off · Tuesdays

Show Columbia ID every Tuesday for 10% off groceries. Accepts EBT/SNAP. Walking distance from campus at Broadway & 116th.

📍 2840 Broadway · View on map ↗

Amazon Prime Student

50% off Prime

$7.49/month with .edu email. Includes free Whole Foods pickup and delivery discounts. Pairs with EBT for additional grocery savings on Amazon Fresh.

amazon.com/prime/student ↗

EBT on delivery apps

SNAP accepted

Instacart, Amazon Fresh, Walmart Grocery, and FreshDirect all accept SNAP/EBT for grocery delivery in NYC. Note: delivery fees are not covered by SNAP and must be paid separately.

Check each app's payment settings to add your EBT card

🌙 Halal finder

HappyCow (vegan & vegetarian finder)

Maps vegetarian, vegan, and veg-friendly restaurants and grocery stores near any location. Useful for finding affordable plant-based options and soy-free alternatives around campus.
